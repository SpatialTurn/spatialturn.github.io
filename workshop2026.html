A two-day workshop at Purdue University covering GIS fundamentals, remote sensing, Python-based spatial analysis, and data curation.

Day 1

  • 1First Steps EveryoneInstall QGIS, set up Jupyter Notebooks, and review the workshop schedule.

Then choose a track

Track A: Working with GIS Data in QGIS · 3 sessions

  • 1AWorking and Teaching with QGISIntroduction to the QGIS interface, loading data, and basic cartography.
  • 2ARaster Data and Remote SensingWorking with raster datasets and remote sensing tools in QGIS.
  • 3ASatellite Imagery AnalysisAnalysis and visualization techniques with satellite imagery.

Track B: Geospatial Python · 1 extended session

  • B1Python Notebook IntroductionNotebooks in Google Colab and the core libraries: NumPy, pandas and Matplotlib.
  • B2Acquiring and Exploring Census DataTIGER/Line shapefiles, the Decennial Census and the ACS, through the Census API.
  • B3Census Data Analysis with PythonCleaning, sorting and summarizing a Census dataset in a notebook.

Day 2

  • 2Putting it all Together: Designing and Building Geospatial Teaching Modules EveryoneGeospatial data types, metadata standards, data management across disciplines, and best practices for sharing and preserving spatial datasets.
